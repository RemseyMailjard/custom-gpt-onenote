01 Overview

NoteBuddy lets you ask ChatGPT questions about your own OneNote notes, and create or update notes, on your behalf and with your permission. This policy explains what that involves.

In short

NoteBuddy does not keep a copy of your notes, does not build a profile of you, and does not share your notes with anyone else.

02 What NoteBuddy is

NoteBuddy is a Custom GPT that connects ChatGPT to your Microsoft OneNote account, so you can find, read, and manage your notes in natural conversation. Access to OneNote is granted through your own Microsoft sign-in, under permissions you explicitly approve.

NoteBuddy is operated independently and is not part of, affiliated with, or endorsed by Microsoft. It acts under your own Microsoft account's permission, within the boundaries Microsoft sets for that kind of access.

03 Data we access

When you use NoteBuddy, it can access OneNote content and metadata within your own account only — for example notebook and section names, page titles, and the content of a page you ask about, read, or ask it to create or update.

NoteBuddy only ever operates within the specific OneNote permission you approve at sign-in. It cannot see your mail, calendar, files, or any other Microsoft 365 data, because it never requests permission to.

04 What we don't collect

  • No permanent copy of your notes. NoteBuddy does not maintain its own database of your notebooks, sections, pages, or search results.
  • No analytics or tracking. No cookies, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics scripts.
  • No credential storage. NoteBuddy never sees your Microsoft password — signing in happens entirely on Microsoft's own login page.
  • No cross-user access. NoteBuddy only ever acts on the signed-in user's own account and has no mechanism to read another person's OneNote.
  • No sale of data. There is nothing to sell — see above.

05 How sign-in works

NoteBuddy uses Microsoft's own sign-in process — the same one you'd see signing in to any Microsoft-connected app. You sign in on a genuine Microsoft page (never a NoteBuddy page) and approve the specific OneNote permission requested. NoteBuddy only ever acts within that approved permission, and never has access to your Microsoft password.

You can review or revoke this access at any time from your Microsoft account, independently of NoteBuddy — see Your controls.

06 Sharing

NoteBuddy shares data with exactly one party: Microsoft, because that is the only way to read or write OneNote on your behalf.

NoteBuddy does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your notebook content or account information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

07 Retention

By default, NoteBuddy retains nothing beyond what's needed to handle your request in the moment. Limited operational records may briefly be kept for reliability and troubleshooting purposes, but never your note titles, note content, or sign-in credentials.

08 Security

  • All communication involved is encrypted in transit.
  • Access is scoped and time-limited — NoteBuddy can only do what your Microsoft account permission allows, for as long as that permission stays valid.
  • Credentials used to operate NoteBuddy are kept confidential and are never exposed to ChatGPT or to you as the end user.

09 Your controls

Because NoteBuddy relies entirely on your Microsoft account's own permission system, you stay in control through Microsoft, not through NoteBuddy:

  • Revoke access any time at myaccount.microsoft.com under app permissions — this immediately stops NoteBuddy from making further requests.
  • Delete or edit notes directly in OneNote, exactly as before — NoteBuddy holds no separate copy that would need deleting.
  • Stop using the GPT at any time; no account with NoteBuddy itself needs to be closed, because none is created.

10 Children's privacy

NoteBuddy is intended for use by adults managing their own Microsoft 365 / OneNote accounts and is not directed at children under 13. It is not knowingly used to collect information from children.

11 Changes to this policy

If NoteBuddy's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
