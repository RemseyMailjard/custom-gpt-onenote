Overview

OneNote Assistant is a custom GPT that lets you manage your Microsoft OneNote notebooks from within ChatGPT. It works through a small proxy service ({{PROXY_BASE_URL}}) that sits between ChatGPT and the Microsoft Graph API — it exists only to reformat requests into the shape Microsoft Graph expects, not to collect data.

This policy explains what the Action can see, what — if anything — is stored outside of Microsoft's own systems, and how you can revoke access at any time.

Data the Action accesses

When you sign in with your Microsoft work or school account, you grant the Action the following delegated Microsoft Graph permissions:

Notes.ReadWrite Read, create, edit, and delete pages, sections, and notebooks in your own OneNote.
offline_access Keep you signed in between conversations, instead of asking you to reauthorize every session.

These permissions only ever reach your own OneNote content — never another user's. The Action cannot read your email, calendar, files, or any other Microsoft 365 data; Microsoft Graph grants each permission individually per app, and none beyond the two above are requested.

What we store

Nothing, by design. The proxy is stateless: it has no database. Each request is forwarded to Microsoft Graph and the response is returned as-is, in the same call.

Specifically:

  • Your OneNote content (notebooks, sections, pages, page HTML) passes through the proxy in memory only, for the duration of a single request, and is never written to disk or a database.
  • Your Microsoft access and refresh tokens are forwarded from ChatGPT to Microsoft Graph on each call; the proxy does not persist them.
  • ChatGPT itself retains your conversation history according to OpenAI's privacy policy, independent of this Action.

Third parties involved

Two other parties are necessarily involved whenever you use this Action:

  • Microsoft — as the identity provider (Entra ID) and the owner of the OneNote data itself (Microsoft Graph). Your sign-in and data are governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • OpenAI — as the platform running ChatGPT and the custom GPT interface. Your conversations are governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

No data is shared with any party beyond these two — there are no analytics services, advertising SDKs, or additional data processors in the request path.

Data retention

Because the proxy stores nothing, there is no retention period to describe on our end. The Azure hosting platform (Azure Functions) keeps standard operational logs — timestamps, HTTP status codes, and response times — for a limited period, for diagnosing outages. These logs do not include your OneNote content or access tokens.

Your controls

You can revoke this Action's access at any time:

  • Go to myaccount.microsoft.com → Privacy → Apps and services, find “OneNote CustomGPT Action”, and remove access.
  • Or, within ChatGPT, open this GPT's settings and disconnect the Action's authentication.

Revoking access immediately invalidates the Action's ability to reach your OneNote — nothing further needs to be deleted, since nothing was retained.

Security

All traffic between ChatGPT, the proxy, and Microsoft Graph is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Authentication uses Microsoft's standard OAuth 2.0 authorization code flow — your Microsoft password is never seen by the Action or the proxy, only by Microsoft's own sign-in page.

Children's privacy

This Action is intended for use by individuals with an existing Microsoft work or school account and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
